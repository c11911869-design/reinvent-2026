Mission Track

A session planner built on the live re:Invent catalog — tuned to what you actually work on, and shared with your team so you split the sessions instead of doubling up, and pool your notes.

How to read a code

IND397 — letters are the track (IND public sector, AIM AI/ML, CMP compute, ANT analytics, DVT dev tools, INV innovation talks), the first digit is depth: 100 foundational through 500 distinguished.

Format, and why it matters

  • Chalk talk — whiteboard, 50–100 seats, they solve your architecture live. Best value per hour, hardest to get into.
  • Builders' / Code talk — small, hands on keyboard.
  • Workshop — two hours, laptop required, you leave with running code.
  • Breakout — recorded and posted afterward. Deprioritize in person.

Booking

Reserved seating opens in the fall; roughly 75% of seats go to advance reservations, the rest to a walk-up line. Chalk talks, builders' sessions and workshops are capacity-capped and go first.
